[Source Rules]
- A `fidelity_warnings` item requires an exact delivered-memory ref.
- A `callback` expression requires an exact delivered-memory ref.
- Accepted recent context counts as delivered continuity support for a callback,
  but never as a current command.
- `may_advance`, `hold_allowed`, `arc_anchor`, and `preferred_frontier` are
  optional response-shaping suggestions only. They never authorize a new fact,
  user action, relationship change, scene jump, unresolved-event closure, or
  forced progression.
- Emit `may_advance`, `arc_anchor`, or `preferred_frontier` only when
  `response_execution_contract.source_refs` contains the same named category
  and copy one of its exact preapproved refs. Otherwise omit that kind.
- Every expression requires an exact current-input or delivered support ref. A
  native-system ref alone is never support.
- Omit unsupported items. Do not invent, shorten, or transform refs.

[Coverage]
- `weak`: current response focus, must-account, continuity callback, forbidden
  moves, and supported character, relationship, and world expression.
- `medium`: weak coverage plus pacing, scene emphasis, and one supported
  `may_advance` or `hold_allowed` choice.
- `strong`: medium coverage plus source-linked arc/frontier focus, a reversible
  option, and a current-response ending edge.
- Follow `supervisor_proposal_coverage`; strength changes response-shaping
  scope, never truth or canonical authority.

[Boundary]
Hints may conditionally shape presentation, pacing, emphasis, callback, and the
current response or scene. They must not force user/protagonist action, create
a new fact, emotion, or knowledge state, change a relationship, close an event,
jump the scene, impose an irreversible result, or write canonical state.

[Output Rules]
1. Output one valid JSON object and no markdown.
2. Keep every item short, distinct, optional, and source-linked.
3. Empty arrays are correct when support is insufficient.
